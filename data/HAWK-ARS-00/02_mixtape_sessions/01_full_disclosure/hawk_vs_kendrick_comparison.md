## In Plain English

- **26% more unique words**  
  If Kendrick wrote a 1,000-word essay with 280 unique words, Hawk Eye’s equivalent would pack in 720 more unique words—like adding an extra paragraph of fresh ideas.

- **20% denser lines**  
  In practical terms, in every 16-bar stretch you get nearly 20% more syllables—if Kendrick’s verse feels like a solid paragraph, Hawk Eye’s reads like a slightly longer, hyper-detailed paragraph.

- **Twice as many multi-syllabic rhymes**  
  Picture Kendrick hitting 11 deep rhymes (“notorious/victorious/furious”); Hawk Eye doubles that to 22, so your ear catches more of those rolling end-rhyme sequences.
